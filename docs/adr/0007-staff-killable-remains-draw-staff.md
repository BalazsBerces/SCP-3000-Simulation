# The Staff can be killed, at great cost, and their remains draw other Staff

The Staff could have been invulnerable, so that walls, hiding and silence are the only defence. We chose instead that a Staff can be killed, but only with great effort: a group with late Gear (metal armour, a Staff-killing weapon), with noise that draws more Staff to the fight. A killed Staff leaves **Staff remains**, which after a few hours start drawing other Staff, ever more strongly, until Staff carry them off at Closing time. Killed Staff are replaced by the Staff's normal growth over Store days.

Invulnerable Staff made late-game Villages feel static: past a certain wall Tier nothing new could happen. Freely killable Staff would turn the simulation into a combat game and empty the night of dread. Killable-but-costly Staff with dangerous remains keeps the night frightening, gives guards and late Gear a real purpose, and turns each kill into a new problem and a new tool: the remains must be hauled away from the Village, or can be carried off as bait to lure a Horde elsewhere or into a trap.

## Consequences

- Staff have health and can die; Sieges and Expeditions can end with Staff remains in the world, which the snapshot, save format and chronicle must carry.
- Staff remains are a noise-like attraction source with a delay and growing strength; the Staff rules treat it alongside noise, and light still has no effect.
- Villagers need a decision to haul remains away or use them as bait, which ties Staff remains to carrying, traps and Beliefs.
- Killing must stay rare and expensive in tuning; if Villages start farming kills, the cost (weapons, wounds, noise, remains) is what to raise, not the killability.
