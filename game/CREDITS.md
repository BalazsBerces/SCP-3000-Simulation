Fetched by `scripts/fetch-models.py` into `game/assets/models`; each is fitted to the core's size of its Furniture type.

- Armchair: [Arm Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/ArmChair_01) by Kirill Sannikov, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/ArmChair_01`)
- Sofa: [Sofa 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_02) by Kirill Sannikov, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/sofa_02`)
- Sofa, two-seat loveseat variant: [Sofa 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/Sofa_01) by Kirill Sannikov, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/Sofa_01`)
- Sofa, large three-seat variant: [Sofa 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_03) by Fran Calvente, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/sofa_03`)
- Chair: [Painted Wooden Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_wooden_chair_01) by Kuutti Siitonen, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/painted_wooden_chair_01`)
- Chair, high-back variant: [Dining Chair 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/dining_chair_02) by James Ray Cock, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/dining_chair_02`)
- Chair, carver variant with arms: [Green Chair 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/GreenChair_01) by Kirill Sannikov, Poly Haven, CC0 (`models/GreenChair_01`)
- Toilet: [Toilets pack: Toilet_Elongated_C](https://opengameart.org/content/toilets) by loafbrr_1, OpenGameArt, CC0 (`models/toilet`)
- Vanity unit (pedestal washbasin): [Toilets pack: Sink_C](https://opengameart.org/content/toilets) by loafbrr_1, OpenGameArt, CC0 (`models/washbasin`)
